About Me

The hands behind alucrochetco

Hi, I'm Alu, the maker behind alucrochetco.

My yarn journey started in 2016 when a neighbour taught me how to knit. Years later, in 2023, I picked up a crochet hook after falling down a YouTube rabbit hole. I was curious to see if I could learn it — and I haven't really put it down since.

I spent my first couple of years making hats, clothing, and little accessories, learning new techniques with every project. Then, in November 2025, I made my first plushie, Daisy. She completely changed the direction of my crochet journey and is still one of my favourite creations.

What I Make

I love creating things that people can wear, cuddle, gift, or simply enjoy. From plushies and accessories to clothing, every project gives me the chance to try something new while creating something made to last.

My Process

I'm mostly self-taught, so a lot of what I know has come from experimenting, making mistakes, and trying again. Every piece is handmade from start to finish, and I pay close attention to the little details because I believe they're what make handmade items feel special.

I don't rush my work. I'd rather spend the extra time getting something right than make something I'm not proud of.

Why alucrochetco?

I started alucrochetco because I wanted to share the joy I found in creating. My goal is simple: to make pieces that bring comfort, spark happiness, and become part of someone's everyday life.

Whether it's a plushie sitting on your bed, a hat you wear every winter, or a gift for someone you love, I hope each piece carries a little bit of the care that went into making it.

Thanks for stopping by and supporting my small business. It truly means the world to me. 🤍
