About Me

Hi, I’m Alu. I’m a 21-year-old self-taught crochet maker. I started crocheting in 2023 after learning through YouTube tutorials and exploring it on my own. What began as curiosity quickly turned into something I genuinely enjoy and continue to grow with.

In November of last year, I began making plushies — and that’s when I really found my creative direction.

My Work

I enjoy making toys, accessories, and clothing, but plushies became my favourite focus because they feel more fun, expressive, and creatively rewarding.

One of my most meaningful creations is Daisy, my very first plushie. She represents a turning point for me and opened up a whole new creative path.

My Process

Every plushie is handmade with care and attention to detail. A single bunny usually takes around 9–10 hours to complete.

I focus on keeping quality and intention in every stitch, and I enjoy the process of choosing colours and stuffing each piece — that’s when it really comes to life.

What I Hope You Feel

I want my plushies to bring happiness, comfort, and a sense of wonder — like something soft and special you didn’t expect to find.

When someone holds one of my creations, I hope they feel happy and slightly amazed.

My Brand

My style is soft, fun, and aesthetic. I want my brand to feel gentle and comforting, but still playful and full of personality.

I’m currently growing my handmade business while keeping that personal, handmade touch that makes it meaningful.

My plushies are made for kids and young adults — anyone who still finds joy in soft, cute, comforting things.
