About Me

Hi, I’m Alu. I’m a 21-year-old self-taught crochet maker. I started crocheting in 2023 after learning through YouTube tutorials and exploring it on my own. What began as curiosity quickly turned into something I genuinely enjoy and continue to grow with.

In November of last year, I began making plushies — and that’s when I really found my creative direction.

My Work

I enjoy making toys, accessories, and clothing, but plushies became my favourite focus because they feel more fun, expressive, and creatively rewarding.

One of my most meaningful creations is Daisy, my very first plushie.

My Process

Every plushie is handmade with care. A single bunny takes around 9–10 hours to complete.

My Brand

My style is soft, fun, and aesthetic — made for people who love cute, comforting things.

I’m currently growing my handmade business while keeping that personal, handmade touch that makes it meaningful.

My plushies are made for kids and young adults — anyone who still finds joy in soft, cute, comforting things.
